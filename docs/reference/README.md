# Reference Map

`docs/reference/` chua tai lieu tham khao va lich su.

## Legacy text

Thu muc `legacy_text/` giu:

- cac phac thao spec cu
- ghi chu lap luan ban dau
- tai lieu thiet ke feature / preprocessing truoc khi co v4

## Cach su dung

- duoc dung de doi chieu va giai thich lich su ra quyet dinh
- khong duoc dung lam source-of-truth ky thuat neu no mau thuan voi `docs/specs/`

## Luu y

Nhiều claim trong legacy text da bi supersede boi audit moi, dac biet:

- claim ve `JPEG bottleneck` nhu history eraser
- claim ve baseline 33 nhu "mien nhieu voi JPEG"
- claim ve local chroma texture nhu feature space sach
